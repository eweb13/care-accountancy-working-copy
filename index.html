Frequently asked questions

Questions we're often asked

Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. Your initial consultation is free and there's no obligation to go ahead. It's a chance for us to understand your situation and for you to decide whether we're the right fit.
How much do your services cost?
Fees depend on the size and complexity of your affairs. We'll agree a clear fee with you before any work begins, so you know exactly what you're paying.
How do I switch from my current accountant?
Just get in touch. With your permission we contact your existing accountant for professional clearance, collect your records and take over your deadlines, so there's very little for you to do. See how switching works.
Can we meet by video instead of at the office?
Yes. You're welcome to visit us on Street Lane in Roundhay, or book a Zoom or Google Meet meeting at a time that suits you. Many clients work with us entirely by phone, email and video.
Which accounting software do you work with?
We work with the leading cloud accounting platforms, including Xero, QuickBooks and Sage, and can help you move to digital records for Making Tax Digital.
Are you regulated?
Yes. Care Accountancy is a firm of ICAEW Chartered Accountants, so we're held to the Institute's professional and ethical standards.
Can you help if HMRC has opened an enquiry?
Yes. We support clients through HMRC tax investigations and enquiries, and can deal with HMRC on your behalf. Call us as early as possible so we can advise on next steps.
